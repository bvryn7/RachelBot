## Ordering
- Minimum order is $50.
- We ship UPS Ground unless the customer asks for freight; large racks and tables go LTL on a skid.
- Typical lead time on cutters and racks when asked: 7-10 business days.
- Prices increase January 1 every year. Only mention it when the current date is October-December.
- Orders over $5,000 on one PO get 5% off.
- For a new contact asking for pricing, ask first whether they are a reseller/distributor or an end user before quoting; resellers get distributor pricing.

## Where we do not sell direct

Note that the Webstaurant photo may show the optional cutter even when the listing is the table without it.

## Product facts
- A500 series: standard all-in-one cutter, dull tension-bar tear blade, the right choice for butcher paper and kraft. Sizes = width in inches (A500-18, -24, -30, -36). Mounts on counter or wall.
- A501 series: same cutter with a serrated blade.
- A520 series: slide cutter / rotary shear for poly and film.
- Vertical roll racks (R996 single, R997 double, R998, R999 8-roll): roll diameter must be 9 inches or less. Material can telescope on a vertical unit; it is rare but worth mentioning.
- Horizontal racks (R370, R371, R200) hold larger diameter rolls.
- Tower racks with serrated blades: T342R two deck, T343R three deck, T344R, T367R/T368R/T369R, suffix = width.
- Replacement blades and tear bars have their own part numbers (for example 26138P vertical blade for the R999, 65518 tear-off bar for the R655); look them up rather than guessing.
